Help & privacy

Local access

Images and ratings stay on this computer. There is no authentication: anyone with access to this session can review them. Do not expose this port, use a tunnel, or share the private folder.

Viewer controls

Focus the image: wheel or arrow keys change slices; drag uses the selected tool. W / P / Z select window / level, pan, or zoom. + / − zoom; R resets. Presets: 1 soft tissue, 2 lung, 3 liver, 4 bone, 5 brain.

Local 2D canvas renderer, not the OHIF engine. Windowing and zoom apply to decoded pixels.

Review guidance

Save a draft and skip if evidence is insufficient. Loaded slices are not necessarily inspected. Drafts autosave locally; final reviews can be revised and earlier revisions are retained. Finalizing confirms an assessment, not diagnostic suitability or inspection of every slice.
